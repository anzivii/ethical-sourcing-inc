Welcome to VerityScore

Search for a company to see its sector and ethical percentage rating.

Ratings are based on analysis of HRDD tier, child/forced labor risk, and sustainability. Higher score = better ethics.

Ethical Ratings Breakdown

80–100 → Very Ethical

60–79 → Ethical

40–59 → Moderately Ethical

Below 40 → Unethical

Additional Breakdown

HRDD Tier

Leader = top performers in CHRB / strong policies + enforcement

Strong = good systems, decent transparency

Moderate = partial systems or inconsistent disclosure

Weak = poor transparency or known gaps

Child/Forced Labor Risk

Low = minimal risk

Medium = some risk factors present

High = significant risk factors

Sustainability

High = strong environmental policies, renewables, waste reduction

Medium = some initiatives

Low = limited efforts / negative impact

Notes

Finance companies are lower physical risk due to fewer supply chains

Unethical scores (<40) include alternative suggestions
